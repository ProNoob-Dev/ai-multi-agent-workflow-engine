# Data Handling Policy

## Client data
Client data must never be shared with a vendor or third party without
written client consent and a signed data processing agreement.

## PII masking
Personal data (names, phone numbers, addresses, IDs) must be masked
before it is sent to any external system, including AI services.

## Storage
Business documents are stored in approved systems only. Do not store
client data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.

## Breach reporting
Any suspected data leak must be reported to the security team within
24 hours of discovery.
