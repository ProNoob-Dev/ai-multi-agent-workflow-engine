# Procurement Policy

## Vendor selection
Purchases above INR 50,000 require quotes from at least 3 vendors.
Purchases above INR 2,00,000 require a formal tender process.

## Purchase orders
No vendor work may start without an approved purchase order (PO).
Invoices without a matching PO are returned to the vendor.

## Preferred vendors
Departments should prefer vendors on the approved vendor list. A new
vendor needs finance verification before the first purchase order.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are Net 30 unless the contract states otherwise.
Early payment discounts may be taken when the saving exceeds 2%.
