# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year, credited
monthly at 2 days per month.

## Carry forward
Up to 10 unused leave days can be carried forward to the next calendar
year. Days beyond 10 expire on 31 December and cannot be encashed.

## Sick leave
12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Approval
Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance for
planned leave. Manager approval is required before booking travel.
