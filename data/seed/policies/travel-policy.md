# Travel Policy

## Hotel stays
Domestic hotel stays are capped at INR 5,000 per night for metro cities
and INR 3,500 per night elsewhere. International stays need prior
manager approval regardless of cost.

## Flights
Book economy class for flights under 5 hours. Business class needs
director approval. Book at least 7 days in advance where possible.

## Local travel
Local travel by cab is reimbursed at actuals up to INR 2,000 per day.
Use company-provided transport where available.

## Advances
Travel advances are available up to 80% of the estimated trip cost and
must be settled within 7 days of returning.
