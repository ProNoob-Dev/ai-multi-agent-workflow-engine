# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Reimbursement limit
Employees may claim business expenses up to INR 25,000 per month without
additional approval. Claims above INR 25,000 need manager approval.

## Receipts
Original receipts are required for every claim above INR 500. Claims
without receipts are reimbursed only up to INR 500 per item.

## Submission window
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
Late claims need finance approval and may be rejected.

## Categories
Eligible categories: travel, meals with clients, office supplies,
software subscriptions and training. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
